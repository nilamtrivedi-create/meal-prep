Day 1

FALAFEL BOWLS

WITH LEMON DILL PILAF
Makes 2 servings

Ingredients

  • Prepped Ingredients
  • Falafel (Page 6)¹³
  • Lemon Dill Pilaf (Page 7)¹
  • Tzatziki (Page 11)¹⁴⁵
  • Crazy Feta (Page 10)¹³
  • Pickled Jalapeños & Shallots (Page 12)¹⁴
  • 6 (or ½ batch) Falafel
  • 1 batch Lemon Dill Pilaf, reheated ~110g
  • Thinly sliced Romaine Lettuce, to taste
  • 4 oz (about ½ cup) Cherry Tomatoes, halved ~113g
  • ½ Cucumber, chopped into half moons ~170g
  • Tzatziki, to taste
  • Crazy Feta, to taste
  • Pickled Jalapeños & Shallots, for garnish
  • Pita Chips (optional)
  • 1

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375ºF.
  2. Place cooked falafel on a sheet tray and bake for 6-7 minutes (or until fully reheated). Construct the Bowls
  3. Place ¾ cup of reheated lemon dill pilaf at the base of each bowl.
  4. Top each bowl with a handful of romaine lettuce, cherry tomatoes, and cucumbers.
  5. Add a spoonful of tzatziki and crazy feta. Top with reheated falafel, pickled jalapeños & shallots, and pita chips (optional) and serve.
Day 2

SUMMER ORZO SALAD

WITH SEARED SALMON
Makes 2 servings

Ingredients

  • Prepped Ingredients
  • Summer Orzo Salad Base (Page 8)²
  • Herby Lemon Vinaigrette (Page 13)²³
  • Summer Orzo Salad
  • 1 batch of Summer Orzo Salad Base
  • 2 oz (about ⅓ cup) Cherry Tomatoes, halved ~57g
  • ½ Cucumber, finely diced ~170g
  • ½ cup (35 g) thinly sliced Radish
  • ⅓ cup (45 g) crumbled Feta
  • Herby Lemon Vinaigrette, to taste
  • A pinch chopped Parsley
  • A pinch chopped Dill
  • Seared Salmon
  • Neutral Oil
  • 2 Salmon Fillets, seasoned with salt & pepper
  • 1 Tbsp (14 g) Salted Butter
  • Juice of ½ a Lemon
  • Chopped Dill, to taste
  • 2

Instructions

  1. Combine orzo base, tomatoes, cucumber, radish, feta, additional vinaigrette (to your liking), and a pinch each of chopped parsley and dill. Season with another pinch of salt and stir until everything is well combined. Prepare Salmon & Serve
  2. Heat a thin layer of neutral oil in a large pan or cast-iron skillet on high heat.
  3. Once the oil is hot and shimmering, add salmon fillets (presentation side down).
  4. Sear for 3-4 minutes without touching the fillets.
  5. Carefully flip the salmon, add butter, turn the heat down to low, squeeze the juice of ½ a lemon over each fillet, and add a pinch of chopped dill.
  6. Using a spoon, baste the salmon with the melted butter in the pan and finish cooking for an additional 2-3 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked to your desired doneness.
  7. Plate on top or alongside the summer orzo salad and serve.

MEDITERRANEAN SALAD

WITH DILLY CHICKEN SKEWERS
Makes 2 servings

Ingredients

  • Prepped Ingredients
  • Spicy Dill Yogurt Marinade (Page 9)³
  • Falafel (Page 6)¹³
  • Crazy Feta (Page 10)¹³
  • Herby Lemon Vinaigrette (Page 13)²³
  • Dilly Chicken Skewers
  • 1 large (8-9 oz) Chicken Breast, diced into 1 ½ to
  • 2-inch cubes
  • Kosher Salt
  • 1 batch Spicy Dill Yogurt Marinade
  • 2 Wooden or Metal Skewers
  • High Smoke Point Oil
  • Mediterranean Salad
  • Romaine Lettuce, thinly sliced
  • 2 oz (about ⅓ cup) Cherry Tomatoes, halved ~57g
  • ½ Cucumber, chopped into half moons ~170g
  • ½ cup thinly sliced Radish
  • 4-5 (or remaining) Falafel, reheated & quartered
  • 1-2 Tbsp Crazy Feta
  • ¾ cup Pita Chips, crushed
  • Herby Lemon Vinaigrette, to taste
  • Kosher Salt, to taste
  • Chopped Parsley, to taste
  • Chopped Dill, to taste
  • Chopped Cilantro, to taste
  • 3

Instructions

  1. Place cubed chicken breast in a mixing bowl with a generous pinch of salt and stir. Add the dill yogurt marinade and stir until the chicken is fully coated. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours. Prepare Chicken Skewers
  2. Assemble chicken on your preference of skewers (wooden or metal).
  3. IF GRILLING (OUTDOORS): Preheat your grill to 400ºF and lightly grease with a high smoke point oil. Place chicken skewers on the grill and cook for around 9-10 minutes, flipping occasionally. Finish cooking chicken skewers on the grill covered (for another 7-8 minutes) or until the largest piece of chicken reaches 165ºF internally.
  4. IF SEARING (INDOORS): Preheat your oven to 400ºF and then preheat a cast-iron skillet on the stove on high heat. Lightly grease with a high smoke point oil. Place skewers on the cast iron skillet and cook for around 9-10 minutes, flipping occasionally. Transfer chicken skewers to a sheet tray and finish cooking them in the oven for 7-8 minutes, or until the largest piece of chicken reaches 165ºF internally).
  5. Transfer chicken skewers to a plate, cover with foil, and allow them to rest for 5 minutes before serving. Prepare Mediterranean Salad
  6. In a large mixing bowl, add romaine, tomatoes, cucumbers, radish, reheated falafel, crazy feta, and crushed pita chips.
  7. Add herby lemon vinaigrette and a pinch of salt, parsley, dill, and cilantro. Stir until well combined.
  8. Plate and serve alongside chicken skewers.

MEDITERRANEAN-STYLE LOADED POTATOES

WITH GRILLED STEAK
Makes 2 servings

Ingredients

  • Prepped Ingredients
  • Tzatziki (Page 11)¹⁴⁵
  • Pickled Jalapeños & Shallots (Page 12)¹⁴
  • Grilled Steak
  • 2 (12 oz) NY Strip Steaks ~680g
  • High Smoke Point Oil
  • Kosher Salt
  • Freshly Cracked Black Pepper
  • Loaded Potatoes
  • 2 baked Russet Potatoes
  • Tzatziki, to taste
  • Crumbled Feta, to taste
  • Chopped Dill, to taste
  • Pickled Jalapeños & Shallots, for garnish
  • 4

Instructions

  1. About 20-30 minutes before cooking, lightly brush steaks with a high smoke point oil and generously season on both sides with kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper.
  2. Allow steak to sit for at least 20 minutes before cooking.
  3. Preheat your grill to 425ºF.
  4. Individually wrap pre-baked potatoes in foil and place on the grill. Cook for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway
NoteYou can also stick your pre-baked potatoes in the microwave for 1 ½ to 2 minutes to reheat. 3.​ Place the seasoned steak on the grill and cook for 4-5 minutes, or until slightly charred. Flip and continue cooking for 3-5 minutes (depending on how you like your steak done). 4 minutes (or 130ºF-135ºF) will result in a medium-rare, 6-7 minutes (or 140ºF-145ºF) will result in medium, and 8-9 minutes (or 150ºF-155ºF) will result in medium-well. 4.​ Transfer the grilled steak to a plate, cover loosely with foil, and rest for 5-10 minutes before slicing. 5.​ Slice into the potatoes, lengthwise, and top with tzatziki, feta, dill, and pickled jalapeños & shallots. Serve alongside the finished steak.
Day 5

GOLDEN CHICKEN & RICE PILAF

Makes 2 servings

Ingredients

  • Prepped Ingredients
  • Caramelized Shallots⁵
  • Tzatziki (Page 11)¹⁴⁵
  • Golden Chicken
  • 1 large (8-9 oz) Chicken Breast, cut into 1 ½ to
  • 2-inch cubes
  • Kosher Salt
  • 1 tsp (3 g) Turmeric ~5mL
  • ½ tsp (1 g) Ground Cumin ~2mL
  • Olive Oil
  • Rice Pilaf
  • 1 Tbsp (14 g) Salted Butter
  • ¼ cup (55 g) Orzo
  • ½ cup (110 g) Long-Grain Rice
  • 1 batch Caramelized Shallots
  • 1 ¾ cups (425 g) Water
  • 1 tsp (4 g) Chicken Bouillon ~5mL
  • 2 oz (or remaining) Cherry Tomatoes, halved ~57g
  • Crumbled Feta, to taste
  • Chopped Dill, to taste
  • Chopped Parsley, to taste
  • Tzatziki, for serving
  • 5

Instructions

  1. In a small mixing bowl, combine cubed chicken, a generous pinch of salt, turmeric, and cumin. Mix until the chicken is fully coated in spices.
  2. Heat a thin layer of olive oil in a Dutch oven, large skillet, or frying pan (with a lid) on high heat. Once hot, add chicken.
  3. Cook the chicken until a golden crust forms. Flip and cook for an additional 1-2 minutes. Transfer the par-cooked chicken to a plate and set aside. Prepare Rice Pilaf
  4. In the same pan, add butter. Once the butter is fully melted, add orzo and rice. Cook until the orzo is golden brown. Add caramelized shallots and stir until well combined.
  5. Add water and chicken bouillon and bring to a boil. Once the water comes to a rolling boil, stir, add the par-cooked chicken, cover, and reduce the heat to low.
  6. Simmer for 30-32 minutes.
  7. After 30-32 minutes, turn off the heat and leave, covered, for an additional 5-10 minutes.
  8. Before serving, top with tomatoes, feta, dill, and parsley, and serve with a dollop of tzatziki.

FALAFEL

12-13 falafel patties

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (200 g) Dry Garbanzo Beans
  • 2 cups Water ~474mL
  • 1 tsp (6 g) Baking Soda ~5mL
  • 2 cloves of Garlic (10 g) ~8g
  • ¼ cup (14 g) thinly sliced Radish
  • 1 Tbsp (10 g) Red Wine Vinegar
  • Juice of ½ a Lemon (20 g)
  • 2 tsp (7 g) Kosher Salt ~10mL
  • ¼ cup (12 g) chopped Cilantro
  • ¼ cup (12 g) chopped Parsley
  • ¼ tsp (<1 g) ground Cayenne ~1mL
  • ½ tsp (<1 g) ground Cumin ~2mL
  • 1 ½ tsp (4 g) Baking Powder ~5mL
  • 1 small Shallot (30 g), diced
  • Neutral, High Smoke Point Oil, for cooking
  • 6

Instructions

  1. Place garbanzo beans, water, and baking soda in a large bowl and cover. Soak for at least 8 hours (or overnight) on the counter. Prepare Falafel
  2. Rinse garbanzo beans and drain well.
  3. Add garlic, radish, red wine vinegar, lemon juice, and salt to a blender and blend until fully combined. Set aside.
  4. In a food processor, combine the soaked and drained garbanzo beans, cilantro, parsley, cayenne, cumin, baking powder, and shallot. Pulse until the garbanzo beans are ground. Transfer to a large mixing bowl.
NoteBe sure to really process your garbanzo beans or your falafels might fall apart. 4.​ Using your hands (or a rubber spatula), mix in the blended wet ingredients. 5.​ Once fully mixed, use a large spoon or cookie scooper to scoop out 2 to 2 1/2-inch rounds of falafel. Allow falafel to rest for 15 minutes (uncovered). 6.​ In a skillet, heat a thin layer of neutral oil (enough to skillet fry). 7.​ Using your hands, shape falafel rounds into patties/disks (lightly oil your hands to avoid sticking). 8.​ Once the oil reaches 350ºF, add falafel patties. Fry for 3 to 4 minutes, flipping halfway. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and season with salt. To Store: Transfer to an airtight container and store in the refrigerator for later use. How to Reheat the Falafel 1.​ Preheat your oven to 375ºF. 2.​ Place cooked falafel on a sheet tray and bake for 6-7 minutes (or until fully reheated).

LEMON DILL RICE PILAF

1 ½ cups

Ingredients

  • 1 Tbsp (10 g) Olive Oil
  • 1 Tbsp (14 g) Salted Butter, divided
  • ¼ cup (55 g) Orzo
  • ½ cup (110 g) Long Grain Rice, rinsed & strained
  • 1 ¾ cups (425 g) Water
  • A pinch of Kosher Salt
  • 1 tsp (3 g) Chicken Bouillon ~5mL
  • Peel of 1 Lemon*
  • 1 Tbsp (4 g) chopped Dill
  • *To peel a lemon, use a vegetable peeler.
  • 7

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil and ½ a tablespoon of butter (7 g) in a medium saucepan on medium heat.
  2. Once butter is fully melted, add orzo and stir. Cook until the orzo is toasted and golden brown.
  3. Stir in rice. Add water, salt, chicken bouillon, and lemon peel. Bring to a boil.
  4. Once the water comes to a rolling boil, stir once, reduce the heat to low/simmer, cover with a lid, and cook for 18-20 minutes.
  5. Remove the rice pilaf from the heat and let it rest (covered) for 10 minutes.
  6. After 10 minutes, remove the lid, take out the lemon peels (you can discard them), and stir in dill and the remaining ½ tablespoon of butter (7g).
NotePlace cooled-down rice pilaf in an air-tight container (within 2 hours of cooking) and store in the refrigerator.

CRAZY FETA

¾ cup

Ingredients

  • ½ Shallot, cut into large dice
  • 1 Jalapeño, cut into large rounds
  • 2 cloves of Garlic (skin on) ~8g
  • Olive Oil
  • Kosher Salt
  • ½ of an 8 oz block of Feta (130 g)
  • 10

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 400ºF.
  2. Place garlic, shallot, and jalapeño on a small sheet tray and lightly coat with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of kosher salt.
  3. Bake for 15 minutes, or until the garlic, shallots, and jalapeños are soft. Allow everything to cool down for 15-20 minutes.
  4. Add feta, roasted garlic (remove the peel), shallot, and jalapeños to a food processor and pulse until finely chopped and fully combined.
NoteTransfer to an airtight container and store it in the refrigerator for later use.
